Solving Conic Problems using Clarabel1

Clarabel can be used to solve conic problems on the form2 2 Linear, Quadratic, Semidefinite, Second-order cone, Power cone, and Exponential cone programs are all examples of conic programs.

$$\begin{array}{rl} \text{minimize} & \frac{1}{2}x^\top P x + q^\top x\\[2ex] \text{subject to} & Ax + s = b, \quad s \in \mathcal{K} \end{array}$$
